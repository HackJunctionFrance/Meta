## Direction contract

THESIS: The console is the first-aid card in the dispatcher's hand: what is happening, which protocol step we are on, what to do next. It refuses the dark control-room dashboard of glowing tiles and gauges.

OWN-WORLD: White card-stock panels on a cool paper-grey ground, near-black ink, one first-aid red reserved for the emergency state and active CPR, a quiet clinical green for "on call". One workhorse sans (system), tabular numerals, 1px rules, 10px radii, no glow, no gradients.

STORY: Red band says an emergency is coming in; the protocol row shows what the witness has reported; the dispatcher answers, talks, sees the scene, ends the call and gets the report.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar (name, simulation notice, connection, alert sound). Status row: state title + timer, live voice status, Answer / End on the right. Left: scene video 16:9, with the three-step protocol strip directly under it (signature move). Right: briefing panel (situation, location, scene, reported facts) then timeline.
